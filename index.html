AI feature activation

There are two AI features launched across AIM and ABS currently: The AI Course Tutor, and the AI Assessment Assistant.
Both features need to be “activated” by the business before they can be rolled out to students. Once you have decided you are happy with the way the features are performing and they are ready to be student-facing, you can activate them yourself for student use.

AI Course Tutor

What is this?

An “always on” agent that can answer basic course/content-related queries (e.g. “What does X term mean?” or “When’s the next due date?”).

What does it enable?

Provide an immediate first line of support, reduce unnecessary escalations, and embed guidance directly within the learning experience.

AI Assessment Tutor

What is this?

Allow students to upload draft submissions into a judgement-free environment and receive structured suggestions for improvement prior to due dates.

What does this enable?

Reduce assessment anxiety, improve learning outcomes, and help students develop clearer expectations of their performance.

Feature 1: AI Course Tutor
  1. Log in to MyAIM.
  2. Select course (where the AI feature is available).
  3. Click on the settings icon in the top right corner.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and expand the “AI course tutor” dropdown.
  5. Tick the “enable” box (it will turn blue).
  6. Click on "save and display".
Feature 2: AI Assessment Assistant

    Set up

    Log in to MyAIM and turn on "edit mode"

    Click into "assessment resources"

    Creating the AI Assessment Assistant

    1. Select "Add an activity or resource".
    2. Select "AI assessment tutor".
    3. Change name to “AI Assessment Tutor – [name of assessment]” (e.g. AI Assessment Tutor – Knowledge Questions Part 1).
    4. Link it to the relevant assessment task.
    5. Click into “common module settings”. In the “availability” section, select “make available but don’t show on course page”.
    6. To move the AI feature into the correct space (i.e. corresponding to the correct assessment task), click on the three dots in the top-right corner, and select “move”. Alternatively, you may drag and drop it to the new location. Ideally, it is adjacent to the submission for that assessment.

    Building the first "AI Assessment Tutor" card

    1. Duplicate the “Assessment Point Submission” card.
    2. Select the three dots above the new card and click “edit settings”.
    3. In the “text” section, enter the generic code (listed below). Note that the text is consistent across all AI assessment tutor cards, except for the “url-of-page-module” text. This is tailored to the specific assessment task.

      4. {GENERICO:type="page-module-card",
      ImageFilename="ai_tutor_card.png",
      url-of-page-module="assessment task specific url",
      Title=" Assessment AI Tutor"}

    4. Hover over the “AI Assessment Tutor grey box (below the cards) that we created in part 1, right click and “copy link”.
    5. Select “save and return”.
    6. Once created, move the AI assessment tutor card between the assessment kit and assessment point submission cards.

    Building the remaining "AI Assessment Tutor" cards

    1. Once you have created one AI assessment tutor, you can simply duplicate it for the other assessment tasks. Once duplicated, you will need to change the “url of the page module” in the generic code for it to link to the specific assessment task AI tutor.
    2. Duplicate existing AI assessment tutor card (using the three dots on the top right).
    3. Click “Edit settings”.
    4. Copy the link address of linked AI assessment tutor and paste it into the “URL page module” section of the code, between the inverted commas (“”).
    5. Click “save and return”.

Feature 2: AI Assessment Assistant

Set up

  1. Select course (where the AI feature is available).
  2. Turn on “edit mode”.
  3. Navigate to the “assessment tasks” section.
  4. Select "add an activity or resource".
  5. Click on "AI Assessment Tutor".

Configuration

  1. In the "name" box, enter "AI Assessment Tutor: [name of assessment task]".
  2. Link it to the correct assessment task (by selecting the assessment task from the dropdown list).
  3. Save changes.

Moving and duplication

  1. Once back to the main page, drag the “AI assessment tutor” under the correct assessment task.
  2. For the remaining assessments, you can duplicate the first AI assessment tutor (select the three dots on the top right, and click “duplicate”), but please ensure to change the “name” and link it to the correct assessment task.
