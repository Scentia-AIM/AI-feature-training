AI feature activation

There are two AI features launched across AIM and ABS currently: The AI Course Tutor, and the AI Assessment Assistant.
Both features need to be “activated” by the business before they can be rolled out to students. Once you have decided you are happy with the way the features are performing and they are ready to be student-facing, you can activate them yourself for student use.

AI Course Tutor

What is this?

An “always on” agent that can answer basic course/content-related queries (e.g. “What does X term mean?” or “When’s the next due date?”).

What does it enable?

Provide an immediate first line of support, reduce unnecessary escalations, and embed guidance directly within the learning experience.

AI Assessment Tutor

What is this?

Allow students to upload draft submissions into a judgement-free environment and receive structured suggestions for improvement prior to due dates.

What does this enable?

Reduce assessment anxiety, improve learning outcomes, and help students develop clearer expectations of their performance.

Feature 2: AI Assessment Assistant

AI Assessment Tutor

Set up

  1. Select course (where the AI feature is available).
  2. Turn on “edit mode”.
  3. Navigate to the “assessment tasks” section.
  4. Select "add an activity or resource".
  5. Click on "AI Assessment Tutor".

Configuration

  1. In the "name" box, enter "AI Assessment Tutor: [name of assessment task]".
  2. Link it to the correct assessment task (by selecting the assessment task from the dropdown list).
  3. Save changes.

Moving and duplication

  1. Once back to the main page, drag the “AI assessment tutor” under the correct assessment task.
  2. For the remaining assessments, you can duplicate the first AI assessment tutor (select the three dots on the top right, and click “duplicate”), but please ensure to change the “name” and link it to the correct assessment task.
